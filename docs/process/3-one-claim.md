# The claim

Write the one claim the piece has to land, in a sentence a stranger would repeat. "You don't need an agent sandbox" is a claim. "ACME puts agents to work for your team, with compute, a vault and approvals" is a feature list with three claims in it, and a reader keeps none of them.

Then write what this reader would not expect. A claim the reader already believes gets nodded at and forgotten. "You don't need an agent sandbox" works on someone who just searched for one because it says the thing they did not expect to read.

Everything else in the piece supports this one claim or goes.

The claim is what the reader takes away. Directions in [step 5](5-directions.md) can carry the same claim from different starts, and a direction may sharpen the claim when readers pick it.

# Guide or tutorial

`--page guide`. A page that teaches one task, written for search and ads, such as "How can colleagues talk to my Claude Code agent?".

## Goal

Get the visitor through the task. The page earns the sign-up by teaching. The product is the tool the steps use, and the task is the subject.

## Who arrives

A visitor who searched how to do something, often as a question. They want the steps and will follow them if they look like they work. They leave when the page sells before it teaches, or when a step is missing.

## Structure

1. **Hero.** The title is the question or the task, the subtitle answers it in one sentence, one call to action.
2. **Sections in task order**, in one narrow column: a heading, a few short paragraphs, a screenshot in the flow of the text. Numbered steps where the reader acts.
3. Other calls to action are text links inside the steps.
4. **Questions** and a closing call to action.

## Rules

`search-intent`, `thin-page` (900 words, 4 sections), `cta-along-page` (hero and end), `problem-wall` (60 words for the opening), `problem-cta`, `repeated-visual`, plus every ads rule. No `mock-per-claim`, `known-mechanism` or `crowd-title`: a guide may explain why something happens and may compare ways to do the task, because that is what the searcher asked.

## Checklist before the reader test

- A reader could do the task from the page alone.
- Every step names what the reader does and what they see after.
- Each screenshot shows the step it sits in.

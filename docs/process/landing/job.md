# Page with a job

`--page job`. A page that answers one question for a visitor already deciding: pricing, a comparison with one competitor, the iceberg page that shows what sits under a chat.

## Goal

Give the one answer fast and in a form the visitor can check: the price for their team size, where we differ from the tool they use, what the product does under the surface.

## Who arrives

A visitor who already knows the product, from the home page, a product page or a search such as "laze vs slack". They opened this page for one answer. They leave when they cannot find it.

## Structure

The job sets the structure:

- **Pricing:** a short hero, the plan cards, the questions people ask before paying.
- **Comparison:** a hero naming both products, a side-by-side table, where the other tool is good and what we add, the questions.
- **Iceberg:** a hero, the picture of the layers, the question it answers.

## Rules

`problem-wall`, `problem-cta` and `repeated-visual`, plus every ads rule. The problem rules apply only to a section marked `[problem]`: a job page does not open on a problem. No length or call-to-action limits: a pricing page can be short and still do its job.

## Checklist before the reader test

- The answer is in the first screen.
- Numbers are ones the visitor can check (a price for their team size, not "from").
- A comparison grants the other tool what it does well, in its own section, then says what we add.

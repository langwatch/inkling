# Product page

`--page product`. A page per product, built with home-page care: for LangWatch, the gateway, governance and Instant Evals.

## Goal

Win the visitor comparing vendors for one product category: show what this product does better, how it fits the visitor's stack, and what it costs.

## Who arrives

A visitor who searched the category ("llm gateway") or clicked through from the home page. They know the category and have other vendors open. They leave when they cannot name one thing this product does better.

## Structure

1. **Hero.** The gain the product gives, a one-sentence subtitle, a call to action and the product working.
2. **Problem**, short, in the buyer's words.
3. **Solution sections**, one per gain, each with a mock, a call to action every second section.
4. **How it fits**: integrations, setup, where it runs.
5. **Pricing, questions** and a closing call to action.

## Rules

The same set as the [ad page](ad.md): `search-intent`, `thin-page` (700 words, 5 sections), `mock-per-claim`, `cta-along-page`, `problem-wall`, `problem-cta`, `known-mechanism`, `repeated-visual`, `crowd-title`, plus every ads rule.

## Checklist before the reader test

- A claim this product makes that the other vendors in the visitor's tabs cannot.
- Each section sells a concrete gain, with its own visual.
- The look and the claims match the home page.

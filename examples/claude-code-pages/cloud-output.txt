examples/claude-code-pages/cloud.md:13:4  landing/problem-wall
  > Why it stops when you close the lid
  why: The problem section is a wall of text. The visitor already lives with this problem; a long telling of it delays the fix they came for.
  fix: Show the problem in two or three short sentences of plain, left-aligned text with an image beside it, the way a blog post opens. Move the rest to the solution sections or cut it.

examples/claude-code-pages/cloud.md:23:4  landing/crowd-title
  > What people hand it before closing the lid
  why: The title describes what other people do. It sells nothing to the visitor and turns the section into a survey of options, where our own option is one card among the rest.
  fix: Title the gain or the pain in the visitor's words ("You've become the relay"). If the section compares options, say which one wins and why, frame ours as the answer, and keep it out of a row of look-alike cards.
  from: Founder review of the Claude Code landing pages, 2026-11-09 (points 3, 9 and 10: "What people hand it before closing the lid" is a stupid title; "The ways people keep it running" and "The usual ways to share it" look useless)
